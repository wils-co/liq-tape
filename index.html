Read-only instrument panel. Quadrant names are lens text for reading the tape, not instructions — the dot's position is the whole of the information. The book is raw depth at a moment, unannotated; funding is the exchange's published hourly rate and premium. The levels are yours, read from levels.yaml and drawn exactly as written — nothing here detects, ranks, or edits them. The volume profile is 15m candles bucketed by price, not a tick tape; notable prints are large recent trades the sampler caught, not a complete flow. Walls are resting size at or above 1.5× the median notional of that side of the book, or 20% of that side's visible notional. CVD is the cumulative signed sampled tape (last ~10 prints per poll), not a full book. Panel ⑧ is the sampler's 12s mark over the lookback; wall lines on it are scaled to the largest wall on screen. Its right edge carries volume at price and, beside it, liquidation notional at price. Liq bands are the exchange's own liquidationPx for open positions in the 200 largest accounts by account value, polled every 120s and grouped within 0.25% — not a model of open interest, and not the crowded mid-size accounts outside that set. Data is the local sampler's append-only files and the official read-only client.
